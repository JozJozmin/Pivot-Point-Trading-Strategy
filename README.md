# Pivot-Point-Trading-Strategy
As a part of my Capstone project at WQU, I'm excited to present a highly effective trading strategy that leverages the power of the pivot point indicator and comprehensive price movement analysis to yield profitable results <br>

**AIM** :<br>
<br> 1. Develop a Profitable Trading Strategy using the pivot points: The primary objective is to create a trading strategy that is profitable in the Indian stock market. This involves identifying entry and exit points that can lead to successful trades.<br>
<br> 2.Assess the Effectiveness of Pivot Indicator: Evaluate the standalone effectiveness of the pivot indicator in the Indian stock market. Determine its ability to accurately identify pivot points, support, and resistance levels to enhance trading decisions.<br>  

**METHODOLOGY** <br>
<br> 1. **Data Collection** : To begin, we imported daily data for the top 100 NSE stocks, covering the period from January 1, 2023, to September 30, 2023. We used this data as the foundation for our analysis.<br>
<br> 2. **Calculation of Pivot, Support & Resistance**: We calculated pivot points, support, and resistance levels using established methodologies. The calculations were performed for each stock in the dataset, providing essential reference points for our trading strategy.<br>
<br> 3. **Breakout Identification** : Identifying the breakouts with pre-defined criteria.<br>
<br> 4. **Average Price Movement Analysis**: We conducted an in-depth analysis of price movements for various timeframes (25, 30, 35, and 40 days) after a breakout.<br>
<br> 5. **Developing trading stractegy** : Defining the entry, exit and risk for the stractegy.
<br> 6. **Backtesting** : We backtested our trading strategy to evaluate its performance in a historical context. The results of our backtesting provided insights into the strategy's profitability, risk-adjusted return, and overall effectiveness.
